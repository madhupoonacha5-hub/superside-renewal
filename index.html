    How to read the numbers

    The portfolio and all outcomes are fictional. Budget usage tracks the client’s creative budget, while account contribution tracks recognized revenue less modeled attributable delivery costs. These measures use separate inputs. Prior-cycle revision effort is observed within the fictional scenario. Alternative outcomes are estimates based on assumptions you can inspect.
